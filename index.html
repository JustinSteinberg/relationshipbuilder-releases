Overview

If you’re reading this,
it’s not too late.

Your texts are a graveyard of good intentions. Three unread from your sister. A “we should catch up” from someone you genuinely like. A question you meant to answer, which was four weekends ago now.

You’re not a bad friend. You just have two hundred conversations and no system.

RelationshipBuilder is a secretary for your text messages. It reads your Messages history, keeps track of who’s waiting on you, remembers what you were talking about, and hands you one person at a time.

What it does

Tells you who’s waiting Questions you never answered. Conversations that stopped halfway. The things buried under four months of scrolling.
Remembers the details What they told you last time, and how long ago. So you don’t have to open with “sorry, crazy week.”
Hands you something to say A few things worth asking about. You write the actual words.
Sends from here It plugs into Messages. Type, send, done. Looks like any other text on their end.
One at a time A card, not a list. Answer or skip, and the next one comes up. Two hundred names at once is just guilt with a scrollbar.
Drops the ones you don’t need Your dentist, your landlord, the group chat from 2019. One click and it stops asking.

Why it works

Two things researchers keep finding:

We regret what we didn’t do. Mistakes sting at first. The things we never got around to stay with us longer, because the reason we put them off stops making sense later. Gilovich & Medvec, 1994, replicated 2022
It means more to them than you think. In thirteen studies with almost six thousand people, a text out of the blue mattered more to the person getting it than the sender expected. The longer the quiet, the bigger the surprise, and the more it meant. Liu, Rim, Min & Min, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2022

Put those together and you get the whole app. The texts you never sent are the ones you’ll think about later, and sending one is worth more than you’d guess. The only thing standing in the way is remembering, and that part a computer can do.

Privacy

It reads your texts, so here is the plain answer about where they go. Nowhere. Everything happens on your own Mac. Nothing is uploaded. No account, no server, nothing tracked.

It won’t write your messages for you. There is one setting that asks Claude for better conversation ideas. It stays off unless you turn it on, it needs your own API key, and even then it only asks what to talk about. A message worth sending isn’t one a computer wrote.

Install

  1. Open the file you downloaded, relationshipBuilder.dmg.
  2. Drag the app into your Applications folder. Don’t run it from the window that opens, or it will forget its permissions.
  3. Open it from Applications. Your Mac will ask whether you’re sure about an app from the internet. Click Open. It only asks once.
  4. The app walks you through letting it read your messages, then asks to use Contacts so you see names instead of numbers.

Permissions

Apple keeps your messages in a protected file, and Full Disk Access is the only key that opens it. There is nothing smaller to ask for. Your Mac also won’t let an app ask in a popup, so you have to switch it on yourself.

The app shows you how. The short version: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, click +, pick RelationshipBuilder from Applications, make sure the switch is on, then quit and open it again. Your Mac only checks when the app starts.
